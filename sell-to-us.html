SELL TO AURUM

Have a British gold coin or collection to sell?

Aurum is interested in selected British proof, graded and collectable gold coins. Tell us what you own and our specialist team can review the coin, certification, condition, provenance and current market context before discussing whether Aurum wishes to make an offer.

01 · IDENTIFY

Tell us about the coin

Provide the year, denomination, series, weight and any Royal Mint packaging or certificate details you have.

02 · CERTIFICATION

Include grading details

If the coin is NGC, PCGS or otherwise certified, provide the grading service, grade and certification number.

03 · REVIEW

Specialist assessment

Aurum can review suitability, authenticity information, condition, comparable market evidence and whether the coin fits our current acquisition requirements.

04 · OFFER

No obligation

If Aurum is interested, we can discuss an indicative offer and the inspection, compliance, payment and transfer process. Submission does not oblige either party to transact.
