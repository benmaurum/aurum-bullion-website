SELL TO AURUM

Have a British gold coin or collection to sell?

Aurum is interested in selected British proof, graded and collectable gold coins. Tell us what you own and our specialist team can review the coin, certification, condition, provenance and current market context before discussing whether Aurum wishes to make an offer.

01 · IDENTIFY

Tell us about your coin

Send us the year, denomination, series and weight, together with any Royal Mint packaging or certificate details.

02 · CERTIFICATION

Add grading details

If certified by NGC, PCGS or another recognised grading service, include the grade and certification number.

03 · REVIEW

Specialist review

Our team reviews the coin, available authenticity and condition information, relevant market evidence and our current acquisition requirements.

04 · OFFER

Discuss an offer

If the coin is of interest, we will discuss an indicative offer and the next steps for inspection, compliance, payment and transfer. There is no obligation to proceed.
