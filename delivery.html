SECURE FULFILMENT

Delivery of your gold coins.

Aurum's historic delivery model used secure insured delivery and specialist logistics providers. Aurum 2.0 retains that private-client approach, with delivery arrangements confirmed for the individual order before dispatch.

United Kingdom

UK orders are intended to be dispatched using an appropriate tracked and insured service. Aurum's historic site offered free UK delivery. The final service, insurance level, delivery window and any signature or identity requirements are confirmed with the order.

International

International delivery can be arranged where legally and operationally available. Import duties, local taxes, customs requirements and delivery charges may apply in the destination jurisdiction and will be confirmed where possible before the transaction is completed.

Specialist logistics

Aurum's earlier website referenced specialist providers including Brinks, Malca-Amit and Ferrari, alongside other accredited couriers. The provider used for a particular order depends on destination, value, insurance and availability.

High-value orders

For higher-value purchases Aurum may require additional identity, delivery-address or compliance checks before dispatch. Alternative secure delivery or collection arrangements may be agreed with the client where appropriate.

Important: Delivery is not confirmed until Aurum has confirmed the individual coin, cleared payment, applicable compliance checks and the agreed delivery method. Do not rely on a generic delivery estimate for a high-value coin order.
