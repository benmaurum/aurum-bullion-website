PURCHASE OPTIONS

Flexible ways to complete your purchase.

Aurum can support different payment routes depending on transaction value, availability and compliance requirements. The final payment method is confirmed before funds are requested.

AVAILABLE

Bank transfer

Bank transfer is available for gold coin purchases. Payment instructions and an Aurum order reference are provided after the order and customer details have been confirmed.

CARD PAYMENT

Debit & credit cards

Debit and credit card payments can be supported for eligible purchases, subject to transaction limits, verification and the payment route confirmed for the order.

Card acceptance and any applicable transaction limits are confirmed before payment is requested. Card details should only be entered through the approved secure payment process.

AVAILABLE · CHECKOUT CONNECTION PENDING

PayPal

PayPal is already available to Aurum as a business payment method. Online checkout will be connected once the required Aurum PayPal integration details are supplied. No account credentials or identifiers are displayed publicly.

PLANNED · COMPLIANCE-LED

Buy with Crypto

Aurum intends to support selected established cryptoassets, initially focused on Bitcoin and approved major stablecoins. Crypto will only be activated through a suitable compliant payment provider after wallet screening, AML/KYC, pricing, settlement and refund procedures are in place.
