PURCHASE OPTIONS

Flexible ways to complete your purchase.

Aurum can support different payment routes depending on transaction value, availability and compliance requirements. The final payment method is confirmed before funds are requested.

CORE

Bank transfer

Bank transfer remains suitable for high-value gold coin purchases. Payment instructions and an Aurum order reference are provided only after the order and customer details have been confirmed.

PLANNED LIVE CHECKOUT

PayPal & eligible cards

Aurum already has a PayPal business relationship. The production WooCommerce site is being designed to support PayPal and eligible card payments, subject to PayPal's approval for precious-metals activity and transaction controls.

COMPLIANCE-LED

Buy with Crypto

Aurum intends to support selected established cryptoassets, initially focused on Bitcoin and approved major stablecoins. Crypto will only be activated through a suitable regulated/compliant payment provider after wallet screening, AML/KYC, pricing, settlement and refund procedures are in place.

Current preview: The GitHub version of Aurum 2.0 does not take money. It records purchase interest and basket selections. Live payment credentials will be connected only in the production commerce environment. Crypto is therefore displayed as a planned payment route rather than a live wallet address. Aurum will never ask a customer to send crypto to an address shown in an unsolicited message.
