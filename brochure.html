GRADING & CERTIFICATION

Condition matters.

Independent grading services assess gold coins using established numerical scales and encapsulate qualifying coins in tamper-evident holders. Grades such as PF69 and PF70 are associated with proof coins, while MS grades are used for mint-state coins.

Always confirm the grading service, certification number and exact grade for the individual gold coin offered.

BRITISH ICONS

Britannia and modern British gold coins.

Britannia has become one of the best-known motifs in modern British precious-metal coinage. Aurum's collection can include proof, graded and special-edition Britannia gold coins alongside commemorative British issues.

ROYAL HERITAGE

Commemorative gold coins.

Royal anniversaries, coronations and heraldic series provide a rich source of modern British gold coin design. Mintage, denomination, weight, finish and condition should all be considered when comparing issues.

UK TAX CONSIDERATIONS

Understand the distinction.

Some UK legal-tender gold coins can receive specific UK tax treatment, but consequences depend on the exact coin and the holder's circumstances. VAT treatment also differs between qualifying investment gold coins and other products.

Do not assume every gold coin or collectable coin has the same tax treatment. Obtain current professional tax advice before relying on an exemption.
