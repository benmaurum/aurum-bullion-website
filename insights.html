EDITORIAL STANDARD

Built for evidence, not noise.

Market reporting should be useful to readers before it is useful to an algorithm. Aurum's publishing workflow is designed around primary sources, explicit dates, clear attribution and original analysis.

  • Use primary sources wherever available.
  • Link material statistics to their underlying source.
  • Separate reported facts from Aurum commentary.
  • Do not present forecasts or past performance as guaranteed outcomes.
  • Review material product, investment and tax claims before publication.
  • Update or correct articles when underlying information changes.

RESEARCH UNIVERSE

Sources the newsroom can monitor

Priority research sources include the World Gold Council, The Royal Mint, LBMA, official central-bank publications, national statistics bodies, regulatory disclosures and other first-party market sources. Reputable financial journalism can supplement primary evidence. Aurum articles should summarise and analyse source material rather than republish third-party articles.
