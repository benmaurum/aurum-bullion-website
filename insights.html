EDITORIAL STANDARD

Built for evidence, not noise.

Aurum prioritises attributable reporting, explicit publication dates and authoritative sources. The automated desk targets at least two relevant items per category per day, while avoiding invented or duplicated stories.

  • Every item displays its source and original publication date/time where supplied.
