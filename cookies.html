Cookies Policy

Cookies are small text files placed on a device when a website is visited. They can be used to make a website function, remember choices, understand how visitors use a site and, where consent has been given, support analytics or marketing.

How Aurum may use cookies

Aurum's previously published privacy notice confirms that the website may collect technical and usage information through cookies, server logs and similar technologies. On the new Aurum website, non-essential cookies should only be activated where the visitor has made an appropriate choice through the cookie-consent control.

Types of cookies

Strictly necessary cookies support core website functions and security. Preference cookies can remember choices made by a visitor. Analytics cookies can help us understand website traffic and how pages are used. Marketing cookies, if introduced, may be used to measure or personalise marketing and should be subject to the appropriate consent requirements.

Your choices

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies using the website's cookie controls. You can also delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect the way certain website functions operate.

Third-party services

Where Aurum embeds or connects third-party services such as analytics, mapping, media or other external tools, those providers may use their own technologies subject to applicable consent requirements and their own privacy information.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the website and the technologies it uses change. The consent controls should be kept aligned with the cookies and services actually deployed.
