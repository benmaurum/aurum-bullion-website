UNDERSTANDING GRADING

Condition.
Certification.
Context.

Professional coin grading provides an independent assessment of a coin's condition. Leading grading services use the 1-to-70 numerical scale, with proof designations such as PF69 and PF70 representing very high states of preservation.

Certification can help with authentication and consistent description, but grade is only one factor. Rarity, mintage, provenance, eye appeal, market supply and collector demand also matter.

UK GOLD & TAX

Understand the tax characteristics.

Capital Gains Tax

Qualifying UK sterling legal-tender coins, including qualifying Sovereigns and Britannias, can be exempt from UK Capital Gains Tax. The treatment depends on the particular coin and applicable rules.

VAT and investment gold

Qualifying investment gold is exempt from VAT under the applicable investment-gold rules. Legal-tender status alone should not be treated as the test for VAT exemption.

Historic and ancient coins

Historic and ancient coins require separate consideration. Their CGT treatment depends on the applicable rules and their circumstances; Aurum does not describe all historic or ancient coins as universally tax-free.

Tax treatment can change and depends on the asset and individual circumstances. This is general information, not personal tax advice. No IHT exemption is claimed.
