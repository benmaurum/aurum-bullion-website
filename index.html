THE ART OF THE GRADE

Condition.
Certification.
Confidence.

Two coins of the same year and design can differ materially in condition. Independent grading provides a structured assessment and, where applicable, encapsulation with a certification reference. Proof grades such as PF69 and PF70 are commonly associated with exceptionally well-preserved proof coins.

Aurum presents grading as one part of the full picture. The issue, mintage, provenance, market availability and price remain important considerations.

FROM PRECIOUS METAL TO COLLECTABLE OBJECT

Gold, transformed.

A collectable gold coin begins with precious metal, but its identity is created through design, striking quality, finish and the historical context of its issue. Proof coins are produced with particular attention to surface and detail, while special editions may be accompanied by presentation cases and certificates.

PROOF GOLD

What makes a proof coin different?

Proof describes a specialist method of manufacture rather than simply a coin's condition. Dies and blanks receive additional preparation and coins are struck to achieve a highly finished appearance with crisp design detail. Modern proof gold is frequently issued for collectors and often supplied in presentation packaging.

BRITISH GOLD

A long tradition, continually reimagined.

British gold coinage spans sovereigns, Britannias, royal commemoratives and modern collector series. Contemporary programmes draw on heraldry, national symbols, anniversaries, literature, music and moments of royal history, giving collectors a broad range of themes and formats to explore.

Know exactly what you own.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you purchase.

What is the difference between bullion and collectable gold?

Bullion is generally purchased primarily for its precious-metal content. Collectable or numismatic coins may also be valued for characteristics such as rarity, design, condition, provenance and collector demand.

Does a high grade guarantee that a coin will increase in value?

No. Grading records condition according to the grading service's assessment. Future value depends on multiple factors and is not guaranteed.

What should I check on a graded coin?

Check the grading service, numerical grade, certification reference, exact coin description and the integrity of the holder. Where possible, verify the certification directly with the grading service.

Do all British gold coins have the same tax treatment?

No. Tax treatment depends on the specific coin and the owner's circumstances. Obtain current professional advice before relying on any tax treatment.

Can I speak to someone before buying?

Yes. Aurum's private client approach is designed to allow buyers to discuss individual coins, specifications and availability before proceeding.
