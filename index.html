BRITISH COINAGE

Centuries of history, held in the hand.

British gold coinage connects precious metal with monarchy, art, national identity and changing periods of British history. From the enduring Sovereign and Britannia to modern Royal Mint proof issues, commemoratives and limited series, each generation has produced coins that record the people, symbols and events of its time.

Aurum concentrates on UK coinage because this combination of recognisable heritage, craftsmanship and collectability gives clients a coherent universe in which to build a collection. COIN GRADING & CERTIFICATION

Condition.
Certification.
Context.

Independent grading gives collectors a standardised assessment of condition and can provide authentication and tamper-evident encapsulation. Services including NGC and PCGS use a numerical scale extending to 70, with proof grades such as PF69 and PF70 representing exceptionally high states of preservation.

Grade does not tell the whole story. Mintage, surviving population, provenance, eye appeal, historical importance and collector demand can all influence the market for a British gold coin. Aurum therefore presents grading as one part of a broader numismatic assessment.

UK GOLD & TAX

A distinctive characteristic of British legal-tender gold.

Capital Gains Tax

Qualifying UK sterling legal-tender coins can be exempt from UK Capital Gains Tax. This is one reason British coins such as qualifying Sovereigns and Britannias can have particular relevance to UK collectors.

VAT and investment gold

Qualifying investment gold is exempt from VAT under the applicable investment-gold rules. Legal-tender status alone is not the test for VAT exemption, so treatment should be considered for the particular coin.

Historic and ancient coins

Historic and ancient coins require separate consideration. Their tax treatment depends on the applicable rules and circumstances.

Tax treatment can change and depends on the asset and individual circumstances. This is general information, not personal tax advice. No IHT exemption is claimed.
