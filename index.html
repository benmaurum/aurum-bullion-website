Independent grading provides a structured assessment and, where applicable, encapsulation with a certification reference. Proof grades such as PF69 and PF70 are commonly associated with exceptionally well-preserved proof coins.

UK GOLD & TAX

Tax advantages of selected British gold coins.

Capital Gains Tax

UK sterling legal-tender coins can benefit from exemption from Capital Gains Tax. This includes qualifying British legal-tender issues such as Sovereigns and Britannias. Aurum identifies the relevant tax characteristics of the coins it offers.

VAT and investment gold

Qualifying investment gold is exempt from VAT. VAT treatment is determined by the applicable investment-gold rules and should not be assumed solely from a coin's legal-tender status.

Historic and ancient coins

Historic and ancient coins are treated separately. Where they are not current UK legal tender, their Capital Gains Tax position depends on the applicable rules, including the rules for chattels. Aurum does not describe historic or ancient coins as universally tax-free.

Tax rules and individual circumstances can change. Information on this website is general information and is not personal tax advice.
